How Multi Checkout works

Multi Checkout

This coupon is applied to the combined current selling price when a student checks out multiple batches together.

Multi Checkout Rules

  • • The student must have at least two different batches in the checkout.
  • • The coupon uses the combined current selling price of the eligible batches.
  • • The combined amount must be within the configured minimum and maximum checkout limits.
  • • Only one Multi Checkout coupon is applied to a checkout, and it cannot be combined with General or Batch Specific coupons.
  • • Multi Checkout coupons are not connected to individual batches.

No Batch Selection Required

No batch selection is required for this coupon type. The student cart determines the combined checkout amount during coupon validation.
